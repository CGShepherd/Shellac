# G3-002 — Quantitative Performance and Critical-Loop Baseline

## Scope

- SCH101 selectable-gain budget at 5 mV RMS input.
- Validated and calculated design margins.
- Component criticality and substitution policies.
- Six measurable critical-loop placement constraints.
- Explicit register of quantities requiring later calculation or measurement.

## Key results

- Worst validated cartridge overload margin: 17.10 dB.
- SCH104 severe-signal margin to project ceiling: 9.87 dB.
- THAT1646 severe-output margin to project design ceiling: 3.85 dB.
- THAT1646 severe-output margin to datasheet capability: 8.95 dB.

Noise and distortion are not represented as falsely precise totals. They remain
open until cartridge source data, final component models and bench correlation
are available.
