# G3-003 — Mechanical Datum and Preliminary Placement Baseline

## Purpose

Create a controlled mechanical model before selecting catalogue enclosures or
committing component coordinates.

## Frozen intent

- Two-box architecture: separate PSU and audio enclosures.
- Audio enclosure: conductive metal; vertically removable lid/base after all
  controls and XLRs are fitted; external width not greater than 300 mm.
- PSU enclosure: conductive metal; sliding or extruded construction permitted.
- Both boxes use removable internal carrier plates where practical.
- Audio inputs remain at the right-side edge and outputs at the left-side edge.

## Mechanical datums

The model establishes carrier-plate origin, audio input edge, audio output edge,
control edge, chassis-bond point, and PSU protective-earth point.

## Candidate trade study

Candidate entries are deliberately classified as plausible, conditional,
rejected, or data-required. Unknown internal dimensions are treated as missing
evidence, not guessed.

## Placement synthesis

The preferred 220 × 140 mm usable board envelope is divided into non-overlapping
functional regions. The synthesis scales only within the minimum 190 × 125 mm
architecture. It is a region plan, not component placement or routing.
