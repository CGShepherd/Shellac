# G3-006 — Enclosure Decision and Carrier-Plate Freeze

## Purpose

Prevent the PCB outline and mounting holes from being frozen against incomplete
catalogue data. The audio enclosure additionally requires vertical service
removal after all controls and XLRs are fitted.

## Freeze evidence

- exact manufacturer and order code;
- authoritative dimensional drawing;
- verified usable internal dimensions;
- boss/baseplate pattern;
- lid/base intrusion envelope;
- panel thickness and machining zones;
- connector and control rearward depth.

## Carrier strategy

The default architecture uses a 2 mm removable aluminium carrier plate with a
5 mm enclosure-edge margin. The preferred 220 x 140 mm PCB is centred on the
carrier unless panel harness or connector geometry justifies an offset.

## Current status

Both audio and PSU selections remain blocked pending complete drawing evidence.
This is deliberate: G3-006 defines how the decision will be closed, rather than
pretending that a catalogue family is already an accepted part.
